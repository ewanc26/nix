PRIVACY NOTICE
==============

Last updated: March 2025

Who runs this
-------------
This Nextcloud instance is operated by Ewan Croft, based in the United Kingdom.
Contact: contact@ewancroft.uk

What this is
------------
This is a private, non-commercial Nextcloud instance, used primarily by me
and occasionally by close family. Access is restricted to a private Tailscale
network (tailnet). It is not accessible from the public internet.

What data is collected
----------------------
Only what is put there: files, calendars, contacts, and anything else
explicitly uploaded or created. No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party
tracking.

How that data is used
---------------------
Solely for the personal purposes of whoever stored it. It is not processed
for advertising, profiling, or anything commercial.

To be direct about it: I have no interest in what anyone else stores here.
Other users' files are their business. Privacy between users is the default
behaviour, not a feature I had to consciously implement. The only circumstance
in which I would access another user's data is for maintenance or migration
purposes — and even then, only as much as the task actually requires.

With that said: I'd recommend keeping original copies of anything important
elsewhere. To quote myself: "it's running on a fucking laptop over wifi." I do
my best to keep it reliable, but it shouldn't be anyone's only copy of
something they care about. iCloud is a reasonable secondary option if you're
already in the Apple ecosystem.

Sharing
-------
Nothing stored here is sold, licensed, or disclosed to any third party. Data
isn't shared with external services unless explicitly configured by the person
who owns that data (e.g. an external calendar sync).

Where data is stored
--------------------
On self-hosted hardware located in the United Kingdom. Nothing goes to
third-party cloud storage.

How long data is kept
---------------------
For as long as the instance is running, or until it is deleted. Files and data
can be removed at any time through the Nextcloud interface.

Legal basis
-----------
This instance is operated exclusively for personal and household purposes by a
private individual. As such, it falls under the household exemption in Article
2(2)(c) of the retained Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (UK GDPR, as incorporated
into domestic law by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018), which excludes
from the regulation any processing carried out by a natural person in the course
of a purely personal or household activity. In plain terms: this is a family
file server, not a data controller.

The Data Protection Act 2018 (c.12) applies insofar as it governs processing
outside that exemption.

That said, I voluntarily apply the principles of lawfulness, fairness,
transparency, and data minimisation set out in Article 5 of the UK GDPR — not
because I'm required to, but because it's the right approach.

Your rights
-----------
Anyone whose personal data is held here has the right to access, correct, or
erase it. To exercise any of these rights, contact me at contact@ewancroft.uk.
